# Contributor Licence Agreement

**Every contributor must agree to this before a pull request is merged.**

## Why this exists

SonarQuest is released under AGPL-3.0. It is also offered under a
separate commercial licence to organisations that cannot use AGPL code —
that dual licence is what funds development.

Dual licensing only works if one party holds the rights to all of the
code. If contributed code were AGPL-only, the project could never offer
it commercially, and the model collapses. This agreement keeps that
option open.

This is the same arrangement used by Grafana, MongoDB, Qt, and most other
dual-licensed projects.

## The agreement

By submitting a contribution you confirm:

1. **You wrote it, or you have the right to submit it.** It is your
   original work, or you have permission from whoever does own it.

2. **Your employer is fine with it.** If you wrote it on company time or
   equipment, you have confirmed your employer permits the contribution
   or has waived their rights.

3. **You grant a licence to relicense.** You give the project maintainer
   a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use,
   reproduce, modify, distribute and sublicense your contribution,
   **including under commercial licence terms**.

4. **You keep your copyright.** You are not assigning ownership. You keep
   full rights to your own code and can use it however you like
   elsewhere.

5. **Patents.** You grant a licence to any patent claims you own that
   your contribution necessarily infringes.

6. **No warranty.** Contributions are provided as-is.

## How to agree

Add this line to your first pull request description:

```
I have read and agree to the Contributor Licence Agreement in CLA.md.
```

One agreement covers all your future contributions. You will not be asked
again.

## If you would rather not

That is a legitimate choice and no hard feelings. You can still help
without signing:

- Report bugs and false positives
- Improve documentation
- Test on your own codebases and report results
- Help others in Discussions

These are genuinely valuable and require no agreement.
